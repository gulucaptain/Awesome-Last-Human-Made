Commentary & critique

Not everyone is mourning.

A graveyard should also hear from those who think the burial is premature.

Scope

Who gets a stone

Included, if a work meets either

  1. Manifesto: it explicitly frames itself as the last, or the end, of a human-made form, in the context of AI replacing it.
  2. Protocol or demo: it does not use the phrase, but proposes or demonstrates an agent-native replacement for a human-centred artifact.

Excluded

  • Works that only share the word “last” (games, unrelated tools).
  • AI generation tools that do not claim to end a form.
  • General agent frameworks already covered by other Awesome lists.

Statuses are editorial judgements and change with evidence. A stone can always be moved back to 🧟 Still alive.
